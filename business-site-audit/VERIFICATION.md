The audit checks one page, robots.txt, and a sitemap through at most 12 public GET/HEAD requests. A separate Cloudflare remote preview returned HTTP 403 for direct `fetch` attempts to 127.0.0.1 and 169.254.169.254. This probes those two destinations, not every possible DNS change. Failed supporting probes are reported as unknown, not a negative or positive finding. The audit cannot establish browser rendering, search index state, traffic, conversions, or a ranking outcome.
